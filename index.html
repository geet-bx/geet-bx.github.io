What I do

Three lenses, one underlying skill: designing behaviour that sticks.

Clinical

EIBI & Independence Training

Seven years of Early Intensive Behavioural Intervention, with a focus on the skills that unlock daily independence: toilet training, feeding and picky-eating interventions, and functional-living programs built on EFL, AFLS, and VB-MAPP (20+ assessments led).

Organisational

Organisational Behaviour Management

Recruitment and retention for clinical teams, plus training systems that help staff communicate with supervisors efficiently. Looking to bring OBM principles to workplaces beyond disability services.

Emerging

Health & Wellness

Applying the same habit-architecture principles that build independence in clinical settings to everyday health and fitness routines — helping people build habits that actually last, in their own communities.

Conference · ABA Australia, Hobart, 2026

Toilet training the learners everyone else gave up on.

My most requested work is the hardest kind: learners with intellectual, developmental, or learning disabilities who've been through years of unsuccessful toilet training elsewhere. I design the clinical program and make every decision remotely — parents run the day-to-day training in their own home, supported by technology that keeps them connected to me without the cost of constant in-person sessions.

It's the same principle behind everything I do: put the expensive clinical judgement where it's needed, and hand the family a system simple enough to run themselves.
